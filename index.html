About CBIT

For nearly five decades, Chaitanya Bharathi Institute of Technology (CBIT) has stood as a beacon of academic excellence and leadership in Hyderabad, Telangana. Established in 1979, CBIT has consistently upheld its reputation as one of South India’s premier engineering institutions, defined by academic rigor and a strong legacy of achievement.

Accredited by NAAC with an A++ Grade, CBIT operates with the highest standards of trust and quality. Its academic strength and faculty excellence are further reflected in its recognition as the 2nd Best Private Engineering College in Telangana by The Week research survey, affirming the institution’s commitment to holistic and future-focused education.

Beyond rankings, CBIT offers a dynamic 50+ acre campus that nurtures ambition, ethics, and innovation. Dedicated to shaping industry-ready professionals, the institute proudly hosts CBITMUN, extending its legacy beyond engineering by inspiring students to become global thinkers, diplomats, and leaders who carry forward CBIT’s values of intellect and integrity.

About CBITMUN

CBITMUN, launched in 2011 by a visionary group of debate enthusiasts, pioneered the Model United Nations culture in Hyderabad at a time when MUNs were still emerging in the region. As the first conference of its scale, it set new benchmarks in diplomatic simulation, professionalism, and academic excellence.

Rooted in the core principles of exceptional debate quality and a superior delegate experience, CBITMUN has grown into a nationally recognized and highly respected conference. Its consistency, intellectual rigor, and professional standards have made it an eagerly awaited conference year after year.

With nearly 500 participants annually from schools, colleges, universities, and professional backgrounds across India—and international participation in previous editions—CBITMUN stands as a diverse and dynamic platform. By bringing together ambitious minds from varied backgrounds, it continues to nurture leadership, critical thinking, and global awareness among future leaders.

Letter from the Core Committee

For over fifteen years, CBITMUN has stood not merely as a conference, but as a living testament to vision, resilience, and the enduring spirit of diplomacy. As we turn the page to a new chapter, we do so with purpose in our hearts and the strength of legacy at our back. CBITMUN 2026 proudly carries forward its guiding ethos: संकल्पेन नेतृत्वम् (Sankalpena Netritvam) — leadership rooted in resolve.

For CBITMUN 2026, we renew our commitment to deeper dialogue, transformative debate, and an experience woven with warmth, dignity, and excellence. CBITMUN stands ready once again to push boundaries, reimagine diplomacy, and create moments that stay long after the conference ends. Mark your calendars for the 10th, 11th, and 12th of April, 2026, three days that aspire not for attendance, but for impact; not for conversation, but for change; not for recognition, but for legacy.

With resolve in our hearts and purpose in our stride, we invite you to be part of a journey that will echo far beyond its conference.


Regards,
